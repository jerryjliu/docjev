# Report assets

Page previews are full-page raster renderings of the authentic originals in `datasets/real-small/v1/originals/`. They are scaled only; source content is not cropped or altered. `manifest.json` records original page numbers and PDF hashes. Source URLs and rights are recorded in `datasets/real-small/v1/SOURCE.json` and `NOTICE.md`. Official marks identify their sources; no endorsement is implied.

LlamaIndex's unchanged wordmark and bundled fonts are copied from `src/jev_docs/web/static/assets/brand/`; see its `NOTICE.md` and `sources.json` for provenance. Font license notices are included beside the font files.
