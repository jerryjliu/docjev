# Source attribution and reuse

This is an independent document-processing evaluation. The U.S. government, its agencies, NFPA, and all other named organizations do not endorse, sponsor, or recommend DocJev, LlamaIndex, Jev, or this study. Original agency marks appear only within authentic source documents to identify their origin.

All original PDFs are complete, unchanged bytes obtained from the official URLs in [SOURCE.json](SOURCE.json) on September 19, 2026. That file records per-document author/issuer, date, rights evidence, hashes and page inventory. Dates may be historical and information may have changed. These are frozen document-processing inputs, not current tax, health, financial, legal, or safety advice. Use the linked agency sources for current information.

The IRS forms, Treasury results and statements, NCUA fund statements, Federal Reserve/Census/BEA/USDA releases, and Federal Register notices are government-authored publications, distributed on the U.S. government-work basis in [17 U.S.C.105](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html#105). Government websites can contain third-party works, so source inspection and particular rights evidence are recorded for each item. No blanket rights assertion is made about everything hosted on those sites.

Materials e033 and e035 were developed by the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC). CDC, HHS and the United States Government do not endorse this project. They are available at no charge from the [CDC archive](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/36072) and [CDC handwashing source](https://www.cdc.gov/clean-hands/media/pdfs/2024/05/329459-A-p.pdf). The archive explicitly marks e033 Public Domain. The complete materials are preserved without substantive changes; see the [CDC agency materials policy](https://www.cdc.gov/other/agencymaterials.html).

The FDA created e034, available free from its [source](https://www.fda.gov/media/74435/download). FDA's [copying policy](https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/about-website/website-policies) permits reuse of its text and graphics unless otherwise noted. This chart identifies no third-party restriction. It was copied September 19, 2026.

USFA/FEMA provided e036–e040. The co-branded e036, e037 and e038 retain NFPA identification. Their inclusion relies on USFA's official invitation to share these free [smoke-alarm materials](https://www.usfa.fema.gov/prevention/home-fires/prepare-for-fire/smoke-alarms/) and [fire-escape handouts](https://www.usfa.fema.gov/prevention/home-fires/prepare-for-fire/home-fire-escape-plans/). These co-branded items are **not** claimed to be wholly public domain or licensed CC0. No separate right to use agency/NFPA marks for endorsement or branding is granted. e039 is FEMA's complete [Ready emergency checklist](https://www.ready.gov/sites/default/files/2021-02/ready_checklist.pdf).

The eight packet PDFs are project-created combinations of complete sources, preserving source-page content. They are not official packets issued by any agency. Packet combinations and evaluation labels are project metadata; they do not alter the source documents' rights. The synthetic dataset's CC0 dedication does not apply to this real corpus.
